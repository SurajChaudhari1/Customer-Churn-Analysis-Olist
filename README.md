# Customer-Churn-Analysis-Olist
End-to-end Customer Churn &amp; Retention Analysis using PostgreSQL and Power BI on the Olist E-Commerce dataset with business insights, revenue loss analysis, and interactive dashboards.

**Customer Retention & Churn Analysis — Olist E-Commerce**
**📌 Project Overview**

This project analyzes customer churn, retention behavior, repeat purchases, delivery performance, and revenue loss for the Olist E-Commerce platform using PostgreSQL + Power BI.

The goal of this project is to identify:

Why customers churn
How much revenue is lost due to churn
What factors impact retention
Which customer segments are most valuable
Business strategies to improve repeat purchases

**📈 Business Problems Solved**
1️⃣ Overall Churn Rate

Identified customers who stopped purchasing for more than 180 days.

Insight
67K+ customers churned
71% overall churn rate
2️⃣ Revenue Lost Due to Churn

Calculated total revenue generated by churned customers.

Insight
R$11M revenue lost due to churn
Retaining just 10% of churned customers could recover R$1.1M
3️⃣ Repeat Purchase Analysis

Compared repeat customers vs one-time customers.

Insight
Only 3% customers placed a second order
Repeat customers spend 1.9x more
4️⃣ Delivery Impact on Churn

Analyzed the effect of late delivery on customer retention.

Insight
Late delivery customers churn at 78.5%
On-time delivery customers churn at 70%
Late delivery significantly reduces review scores
5️⃣ Review Score vs Churn

Studied how customer ratings affect repeat purchases.

Insight
1-star reviewers have the highest churn rate
Every improvement in customer experience reduces churn risk
6️⃣ State-wise Churn Analysis

Identified regions with the highest churn rate.

Insight
Northern states showed the highest churn
Logistics & delivery delays are major factors
